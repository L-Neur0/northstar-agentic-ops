04 The problem today

Today, mapping one process for AI takes 24 person-hours and 2–3 weeks

Two AI specialists run a half-day workshop with two employees, then spend about a day writing it up and chasing numbers. One process at a time, so discovery is capped by the size of the AI team.

05 The solution

The AI Use Case Discovery Agent turns what employees know into a baseline the AI team can act on

The agent runs the interview for every employee at the same time. Specialists review the best candidates instead of collecting them: discovery goes from a few workshops to the whole company.

In

The employee20–30 minutes, in their own words, from a link
Their documentsoptional: procedures, maps, Excel. AI reads, they confirm
AI Use Case Discovery Agent A guided interview with a fixed form behind it

Like a good consultant: asks, listens, follows up, and fills in the same standard form every time.

Through a guided chat, the agent helps an employee share what they know, then select and map a workflow of their own that is worth transforming with AI.
